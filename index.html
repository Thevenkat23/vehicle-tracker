Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my vehicle data stored?
Everything you enter is saved strictly on your local phone storage using an encrypted SQLite database. No data is sent to external cloud servers.
How does the ML Kit OCR bill reader work?
Google ML Kit text recognition runs entirely on your device processor. It reads total cost, date, and fuel amount directly from receipt images without uploading them anywhere.
Does the app require an internet connection?
No! VehiMinder works 100% offline. You can log services, track fuel stops, and view analytics in remote areas without signal.
