Fuel & mileage

Service history

What was done, what it cost, which garage. Set a schedule by months or kilometres — whichever comes first.

Oil monitor

Set it once at 16,000 km with a 2,000 km interval. Every fuel log updates the countdown from your real odometer.

Documents & expiry

Trips

FAQ

Questions people actually ask.

Does Vehicle Tracker need an internet connection?

No. Logging fuel, service, oil changes, documents and trips all works fully offline. Bill scanning also runs entirely on your device, using on-device text recognition — the photo never leaves your phone.

Do I need to create an account?

No sign-up, no login, no server. Your records live in the app on your phone. You choose if and when to back them up.

What vehicles and fuel types does it support?

Bikes, scooters, cars and EVs, in petrol, diesel, CNG, hybrid or electric. Electric vehicles log charging in kWh and get km/kWh instead of km/L.

How is cost per kilometre calculated?

Fuel and service spending are added up and divided by the distance you actually covered, measured between full tanks — not an estimate off the brochure figure.

Can I back up my data?

Yes — export to a file you keep, or rely on your own Google Drive through Android's built-in backup. Either way, it's your account and your storage, not ours.

Is an iPhone version coming?

It's planned. Vehicle Tracker is available on Android today; the iPhone app is in the works.

Is it free?

Yes.
