Fuel & mileage

Tap ₹500, pick your station brand and payment method, done. It remembers your last price and estimates the odometer. Mileage is calculated only between full tanks, so the number means something.

Service history

What was done, what it cost, which garage. Set a schedule by months or kilometres — whichever comes first.

Oil monitor

Set it once at 16,000 km with a 2,000 km interval. Every fuel log updates the countdown from your real odometer.

Documents & expiry

RC, PUC and insurance numbers with expiry dates. A warning a week before, so you're never caught out at a checkpost.

Trips

Route, stops, distance — plus fuel, tolls, food and everything else, totalled per trip.

Spending reports

Overview, fuel, service, trips and costs broken down per vehicle — so you can see exactly where the rupees go, month to month. Export any report to a branded PDF or CSV to save, print or share.

Bill scanning

Photograph a petrol, service or insurance bill. Text is read on your device and the fields fill themselves in — you just check and save.

Notifications

Oil, service and document reminders land on your phone right when they matter — generated on your device.

App lock

Never lose a record

Backups that just happen.

Your logbook is only useful if it's safe. VehiMinder backs itself up on a schedule you choose — to a folder on your own phone, no cloud account needed.

Automatic, on your schedule

Pick daily, weekly or monthly — or turn it off. VehiMinder writes a full backup of every vehicle, log, document and setting to Downloads/VehiMinder on your phone, all on its own.

Back up now, any time

Want a snapshot before a long trip or a phone change? One tap makes an instant backup you can find in your Files app.

Restore in seconds

New phone or a fresh install? Point VehiMinder at a backup file and everything comes straight back — vehicles, history and all.

Yours, and only yours

Backups are plain files on your device — no account, no server, nothing uploaded to us. Keep them, move them to a USB drive, or send them to your own Google Drive. Your call, every time.

FAQ

Questions people actually ask.

Does VehiMinder need an internet connection?

No. Logging fuel, service, oil changes, documents and trips all works fully offline. Bill scanning also runs entirely on your device, using on-device text recognition — the photo never leaves your phone.

Do I need to create an account?

No sign-up, no login, no server. Your records live in the app on your phone. You choose if and when to back them up.

What vehicles and fuel types does it support?

Bikes, scooters, cars and EVs, in petrol, diesel, CNG, hybrid or electric. Electric vehicles log charging in kWh and get km/kWh instead of km/L.

How is cost per kilometre calculated?

Fuel and service spending are added up and divided by the distance you actually covered, measured between full tanks — not an estimate off the brochure figure.

Can I back up my data?

Yes — VehiMinder can back up automatically on a daily, weekly or monthly schedule to a folder on your phone, and you can back up instantly any time. Restore from a backup file whenever you need to. It's your device and your storage, not ours.

What happens when I get a new phone?

Your data moves with you — no account needed. Back up to a file on your old phone (or rely on Android's built-in backup to your own Google Drive), install VehiMinder on the new phone, then restore from that file. Every vehicle, fuel log, service record and document comes straight back.
